# Prospective Clinical Validation Plan for AI Models

## 1. Objective
Establish a formal, ethically approved clinical validation pathway before any machine learning model may transition from **Mode B (Experimental Research)** to **Mode C (Clinically Validated Screening)**.

---

## 2. Validation Phases

### Phase 1: Ethical & Institutional Review (IEC/IRB)
- Obtain formal Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC) approval.
- Ensure informed consent protocols comply with the DPDP Act 2023 and ICMR guidelines for biomedical research.

### Phase 2: Multi-Centric Clinical Cohort Assembly
- Recruit a representative cohort of minimum 2,000 diverse participants across multiple clinical centers in India.
- Include balanced representation of reproductive ages (post-adolescent to perimenopause).
- Rigorously document confirmed Rotterdam criteria diagnoses by board-certified endocrinologists.

### Phase 3: External Validation & Calibration
- Test model calibration using reliability curves and Platt scaling / isotonic regression.
- Target clinical metrics:
  - Screening Sensitivity: ≥ 92% (to minimize missed referral cases)
  - Specificity: ≥ 85% (to prevent clinical workflow overload)
  - Subgroup parity: Verify no performance disparity across demographic or BMI subgroups.

### Phase 4: Regulatory Review (CDSCO)
- File formal Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) submission under CDSCO Medical Device Rules.
- Complete clinical safety documentation, user interface hazard analysis, and post-market surveillance plans.
