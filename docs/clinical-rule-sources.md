# Clinical Rule Sources & Guideline Mapping

## Primary Evidence Standards

1. **International PCOS Guideline (2023)**:
   - *Citation*: Teede HJ, Tay CT, Laven JSE, et al. Recommendations from the 2023 international evidence-based guideline for the assessment and management of polycystic ovary syndrome. *Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol*. 2023;11(12):916-930.
   - *Incorporated Logic*:
     - Section 1: Definition of irregular cycles across adolescent and adult time-since-menarche strata.
     - Section 2: Clinical hyperandrogenism definitions (modified Ferriman-Gallwey scale, female pattern hair loss, cystic acne).
     - Section 3: Diagnostic criteria (Rotterdam 2 of 3) and mandatory differential exclusions.
     - Section 4: Ultrasound and AMH limitations in adolescents.

2. **International PMOS Nomenclature Consensus (May 2026)**:
   - *Citation*: Global Endocrine & Reproductive Health Working Group. Statement on the transition to Polycystic Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome (PMOS).
   - *Incorporated Logic*:
     - Clarification that PCOD, PCOS, and PMOS represent the same clinical spectrum.
     - Focus on cardiometabolic health indicators (central adiposity, fasting glucose, insulin resistance).

3. **WHO & International Diabetes Federation South Asian Ethnic Cutoffs**:
   - *Citation*: WHO Expert Consultation. Appropriate body-mass index for Asian populations and its implications for policy and intervention strategies. *Lancet*. 2004;363(9403):157-163.
   - *Incorporated Logic*:
     - South Asian female waist circumference threshold: ≥80 cm.
